Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rent a bike? +

Choose a bike, check its details, complete your profile, select your date and time, and complete the payment.

Is verification required? +

Required profile and identity verification will be completed before eligible bookings can be confirmed.

How does pickup work? +

Pickup details depend on the owner's selected hand-over option and will be shown to the customer at the appropriate stage.

Is bike delivery available? +

Delivery can be enabled or disabled by the bike owner. If available, the customer will see the delivery option during booking.

Can I cancel my booking? +

Cancellation availability and refund rules will depend on the final RentoRide cancellation policy.
